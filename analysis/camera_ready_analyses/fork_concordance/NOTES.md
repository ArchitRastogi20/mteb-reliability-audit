## Per-fork headlines

Coverage threshold: a model "covers" a fork if >= 50% of the fork's tasks
have a usable score in the raw-results clone. Rank agreement (Kendall tau-b)
is computed on the covered subset of the curated 25-model roster, against
(i) the global 18-task multilingual aggregate and (ii) our per-language
retrieval average for the fork's language.

- **MTEB-French**: covers only **8/25** curated models (40/578 models in the
  whole clone). Its full-benchmark ranking is only weakly related to either
  reference (tau = 0.36 vs global, 0.14 vs per-language, n = 8): the fork
  measures something largely different from both, on the minority of models
  it covers. Retrieval-only overlap is underpowered (n = 5).
- **FaMTEB**: covers **6/25** curated models (26/578 total); overlap with the
  per-language reference is n = 5 → **underpowered**, no tau reported. The
  fork cannot even be used to rank a deployment roster of this size.
- **ruMTEB**: covers **10/25** curated models (41/578 total). Full-benchmark
  ranking agrees more with the global aggregate (tau = 0.78) than with our
  per-language retrieval ranking (0.56) — expected, since the full fork mixes
  classification/STS/clustering. The like-for-like **retrieval-only** fork
  score agrees *more* with our per-language ranking (tau = 0.82) than with
  the global aggregate (0.69), n = 10.
- **C-MTEB**: covers **7/25** curated models (52/578 total). Full-benchmark
  ranking agrees *perfectly* with our per-language Chinese ranking
  (tau = 1.00) and slightly less with the global aggregate (0.90), n = 7.
  Retrieval-only (n = 8): 0.86 vs per-language, 0.93 vs global.
- **ArabicMTEB**: locally unavailable — no registry entry, nothing to score.
- **VN-MTEB**: covers only **5/25** curated models (18/578 in the whole
  clone) → **underpowered** for any rank-agreement estimate.

**Overall**: voluntary-submission sparsity dominates. No dedicated fork
covers more than 10 of the 25 deployment-relevant models, and **none of the
five hidden-failure models (granite-311m, harrier-0.6b, Seed1.6-embed,
inf-retriever-v1, granite-97m-r2) meets the full-benchmark coverage
threshold on any fork** — the models for which the global aggregate is most
misleading are exactly the ones the dedicated benchmarks do not cover.
(One partial exception: inf-retriever-v1 covers 100% of the *retrieval*
tasks of the French and Chinese forks, but < 25% of their full task lists.)
Where a fork is estimable and compared like-for-like (retrieval-only),
its ranking agrees at least as much with our per-language ranking as with
the global aggregate (ruMTEB: 0.82 vs 0.69; C-MTEB full: 1.00 vs 0.90),
i.e. the forks corroborate rather than replace the per-language analysis —
but only for the small model subset their maintainers happened to evaluate.

## Method details and caveats

- **Scoring**: fork score per model = unweighted mean of task `main_score`s
  over the fork's tasks present in the clone; within a task, subsets are
  averaged first, then splits. Split policy: prefer `test`; if absent, use
  the task's declared eval splits found in the file (handles the dev-only
  C-MTEB retrieval tasks). Subset policy: restrict to the benchmark's
  declared `hf_subsets` (e.g. `fr` for multilingual tasks inside the French
  fork); fallback to language-prefix matching on the `languages` field, then
  to all subsets.
- **Revision**: for each model directory the lexicographically last revision
  subdirectory is used (same convention as the existing extraction pipeline).
- **Results-clone size**: 578 model directories (a 579th entry in
  `results/` is a stray helper script, not a model). Baseline directories
  (BM25 etc.) are included in the whole-clone counts.
- **References**: the global reference (`mteb_agg`) is the 18-task
  multilingual retrieval aggregate; the per-language reference
  (`lang_avg_ndcg`) is retrieval nDCG@10. The **retrieval-only** fork variant
  is therefore the like-for-like comparison; the full-benchmark variant is
  what a fork leaderboard actually shows practitioners.
- **FaMTEB overlap**: 6 curated models are covered but `ml-e5-small` has no
  Persian row in `lang_avg_per_model.csv`, so the joint overlap is n = 5.
- **Model mapping**: all 25 curated roster ids matched a results directory
  exactly (`<org>__<name>`, case-insensitive); see
  `curated_model_mapping.csv`. `granite-97m-r2` (extended tier) was tracked
  additionally for hidden-failure coverage only.
- **Statistical power**: taus are computed on n = 7–10 models; treat them as
  descriptive. Overlaps with n < 6 are marked `underpowered` and no tau is
  reported.
- Coverage-thresholded means (>= 50%) are not strictly comparable across
  models with different covered-task subsets; most covered models are at or
  near 100% coverage (see `fork_model_coverage.csv`), which limits this
  concern.
